Financial District, Downtown Los Angeles

777 Tower is the defining anchor
of the downtown Los Angeles skyline

Designed by César Pelli and completed in 1991, 777 Tower rises 52 stories and 725 feet above the Financial District of downtown Los Angeles — offering over 1,025,000 square feet of premier Class A office space. Its sculpted white metal-and-glass facade and distinctive crown have made it one of the most recognizable silhouettes on the LA skyline.

A LEED Gold–certified landmark, 777 Tower provides an unrivaled environment for the city's most distinguished tenants — combining architectural excellence with immediate access to the Metro network, Grand Avenue cultural institutions, and the full depth of downtown Los Angeles.
